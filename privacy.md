# Kepzi Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective date: 26 October 2026. Applies to Kepzi 1.0 for macOS. Operator: Shucheng Wang (王书成). Contact: **admin@qiaboluo.com**.

## 1. Your library and local storage

Kepzi stores prompts, templates, workspaces, groups, tags, favorites and settings on your Mac. Drafts and template field values may also be stored locally for recovery. No Kepzi account is required. The app does not provide an AI model or send your prompts to an AI provider.

Local activity history is separate from online analytics. It can contain search terms, action times, item or workspace identifiers and outcomes. Its retention settings apply to activity history. The library separately maintains usage counts and copy-event metadata; turning activity history off does not necessarily remove those separate records. Use the controls shown in the app, and keep exports or backups where appropriate.

## 2. Optional iCloud synchronization

Pro iCloud sync is optional. When enabled, library content and organization are synchronized through your own private iCloud database. CloudKit payload fields use Apple's encrypted-record storage. Activity history, unsaved drafts and local shortcut bindings are not part of the library synchronization payload. Available storage and service operation depend on your iCloud account.

Kepzi uses the private database rather than a public community library. Your private iCloud records are not available to the operator through the CloudKit developer console. Changing iCloud accounts pauses sync to protect the existing library association. Turning sync off does not delete local data or automatically remove separate iCloud copies.

## 3. Apple purchases

Apple processes the optional Pro one-time purchase and purchase restoration. Kepzi reads verified purchase and entitlement information needed to enable Pro. The app does not receive payment-card details. Your Apple purchase account may be different from your iCloud account. Apple's privacy and purchase policies also apply.

## 4. Optional online usage analytics

Online analytics is off by default and requires your choice in the app. If enabled, predefined product-interaction, performance and diagnostic events are sent to the PostHog service in the EU. Events can include feature and action names, outcomes, durations, bounded counts, app version, error categories, and random installation and session identifiers. These identifiers can link events from one installation; they are not a name or email address, and should not be described as complete anonymity.

The event schema excludes prompt content, prompt titles, search text, clipboard content, file paths, window titles and Apple or iCloud account identity. Online analytics does not automatically record the screen and is not used for cross-company advertising tracking. Network service infrastructure necessarily receives connection information such as a source IP address; the app does not put an IP address in its analytics event payload.

The local pending queue keeps at most 1,000 events for up to seven days. Turning analytics off stops new collection, clears pending local events and cancels sending. It does not automatically delete events already received by the service. Service-side retention and backups are separate from the local queue. Contact the operator for questions or a request concerning previously submitted information.

## 5. Clipboard, feedback and support

Copying or inserting text uses the macOS clipboard and the selected system text service. Other software with clipboard access, and features such as Universal Clipboard, may process copied text. macOS manages any permissions required by the selected feature.

Feedback drafts stay local until you choose to copy, export or send them separately. Screenshots and silent screen recordings are captured only when you start that action. There is no automatic submission of feedback drafts. If you email support, the operator receives the message, your email address and attachments you choose to send; the email providers also process that communication. Please do not include passwords, payment details or unrelated private content.

## 6. Choices and separate copies

You can leave online analytics and iCloud sync disabled, control local activity history, export your library and manage items in Recently Deleted. Kepzi does not add a separate encryption layer to local databases or export files. System security, the chosen storage location and any backup software affect those copies. Deleting data in the app does not automatically delete independent exports, email attachments or system backups.

For support or privacy questions, contact **admin@qiaboluo.com**. If these practices change, this page will be updated with the applicable date and version.

资料库、模板、分组、标签、收藏、设置和恢复草稿主要保存在本机。Kepzi不提供AI模型，也不把提示词发送给AI提供方。本地活动历史与在线统计独立；活动历史可能记录搜索词、操作时间、条目或工作区标识及结果。资料库另行保留使用计数和复制事件元数据，关闭活动历史不等于删除所有此类记录。

Pro的iCloud同步为可选功能，使用用户自己的私有iCloud数据库和Apple加密载荷字段，同步资料库内容与组织信息，不同步本地活动历史、未保存草稿和本地快捷键。容量取决于iCloud账户；更换账户时暂停同步，避免自动把原资料上传到另一账户。关闭同步不会自动删除已有本地或云端副本。

Apple处理购买和恢复，应用读取经验证的购买及权益信息，不接收银行卡资料。购买账户与iCloud账户可以不同。

在线统计默认关闭，开启后向欧盟PostHog服务发送预定义的产品使用、性能和诊断事件，包括功能、动作、结果、时长、有限计数、应用版本、错误类别及随机安装/会话标识。随机标识可以关联同一次安装的事件，不等于完全匿名。事件不含提示词正文、标题、搜索文字、剪贴板、文件路径、窗口标题及Apple/iCloud账户身份；不自动录屏，不用于跨公司广告跟踪。网络服务仍会接收连接所必需的信息，例如来源IP。

本地待发送队列最多保留1000条、最长7天。关闭统计会停止新采集、清空待发送事件并取消发送，但不会自动删除服务已经收到的事件；服务端保留及备份与本地队列不同。对既有信息有疑问或请求，请联系运营者。

复制和插入会使用系统剪贴板及所选文字服务；其他具备剪贴板访问能力的软件或通用剪贴板也可能处理文字。反馈草稿不会自动发送，截图或无声录屏仅在用户主动操作后生成。通过邮箱联系支持时，运营者及邮件服务商会处理用户主动发送的邮箱、正文和附件，请勿发送密码、支付资料或无关私人内容。

可以控制统计、同步和本地活动历史，导出资料库并管理最近删除。应用不对本地数据库或导出文件另加一层加密。应用内删除不会自动删除独立导出、邮件附件或系统备份。如相关做法变化，本页会更新日期和适用版本。
