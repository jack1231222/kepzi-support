# Kepzi Support / 使用支持

Kepzi is a personal prompt library for macOS 27 or later. It stores and organizes your own text; it does not provide an AI model or chat service.

Official website: [kepzi.app](https://kepzi.app/) · [Help](https://kepzi.app/help/)

## Getting started

Create a prompt, enter your text and save it. Use workspaces, groups, favorites and search to find it again. Template fields let you reuse a prompt with different details. Copy the result, or use the macOS **Insert with Kepzi** Services command in a compatible text editor. Press Return or double-click the selected prompt to insert; canceling leaves the target document unchanged.

Free includes 20 prompts, up to 2 templates, 1 workspace, 1 custom group and 7 days of usage insights. Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase with no subscription or automatic renewal. It removes product quantity limits and adds duplication, custom shortcuts, aliases and optional iCloud sync. The current price is shown by Apple before purchase. Family Sharing is not available at launch.

## Purchases and iCloud

Use **Restore Purchases** in the purchase window while signed in with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Your purchase account and iCloud account may be different. iCloud sync requires Pro, a signed-in iCloud account and available iCloud storage. Turning sync on or off does not itself delete your local library. If iCloud storage is full or an account changes, follow the status shown in the app before resuming sync.

## Your data

Use the app's export controls to keep an independent copy of your library. Check the destination and export contents before moving to another computer. Recently Deleted lets you restore removed prompts before permanent removal. Existing exports and system backups are separate copies and are not automatically removed when data is deleted in Kepzi.

## Contact

Email **admin@qiaboluo.com** for support or a privacy request. Include your Kepzi version, macOS version and a short description of the issue. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or a private prompt library. Screenshots or sample text should contain only information you want to share.
